# SaaS Go-to-Market Strategy Builder

Generate complete GTM strategies for SaaS products in the LegacyAI ecosystem including ProfileFlip, NeighborlyAI, and Sympatico.

## Lean Canvas Generation

Create detailed Lean Canvas with:
1. **Problem**: 3 specific pain points
2. **Solution**: Concrete features addressing each problem
3. **Key Metrics**: SMART KPIs (specific numbers)
4. **Unique Value Proposition**: Clear, compelling, differentiated
5. **Unfair Advantage**: Defensible moats
6. **Channels**: Specific growth channels with tactics
7. **Customer Segments**: Detailed personas with TAM/SAM/SOM
8. **Cost Structure**: Detailed breakdown with real numbers
9. **Revenue Streams**: Pricing models with projections

## Financial Modeling

Generate models including:
- MRR/ARR projections
- CAC and LTV calculations
- Burn rate and runway
- Unit economics
- Cohort analysis templates
- Sensitivity analysis

## GTM Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Months 1-3)
- MVP development milestones
- Alpha/beta user acquisition
- Content strategy foundation
- Community building

### Phase 2: Launch (Months 4-6)
- Product hunt strategy
- PR and media outreach
- Influencer partnerships
- Early bird pricing

### Phase 3: Growth (Months 7-12)
- Paid acquisition scaling
- Partnership development
- Feature expansion
- International expansion

## Competitive Analysis Framework

- Feature comparison matrices
- Pricing strategy positioning
- SWOT analysis
- Battle cards for sales

## Output Standards

All deliverables must be:
- Copy-paste ready
- Include specific metrics/numbers
- Actionable (no generic advice)
- Formatted for immediate use
- Include implementation timelines
